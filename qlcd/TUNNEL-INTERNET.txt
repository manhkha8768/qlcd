========================================================================
  DUA HE THONG RA INTERNET BANG CLOUDFLARE TUNNEL
  Cach de nhat: khong can thue may chu, khong mo cong router
========================================================================


CACH NAY HOAT DONG THE NAO
------------------------------------------------------------------------
He thong van chay tren may tinh o cong ty nhu binh thuong.
Cloudflare Tunnel tao mot duong ham tu may do ra internet, va cap cho
anh mot dia chi web co san HTTPS.

Uu diem so voi thue may chu:
  - Khong ton tien
  - Khong phai mo cong tren router hay xin phong IT mo tuong lua
  - Tu co HTTPS, khong phai cai chung chi
  - Dia chi may chu that KHONG bi lo ra internet

Nhuoc diem:
  - May tinh o cong ty phai bat lien tuc. Tat may la khong ai vao duoc.
  - Duong truyen phu thuoc mang cua cong ty


BUOC 0. HOAN TAT PHAN NOI BO TRUOC
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chua dang nhap duoc o chinh may minh thi CHUA lam buoc nay.
Phai vao duoc http://localhost:3000 va doi mat khau admin xong da.


BUOC 1. DOI MAT KHAU ADMIN
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dang nhap admin/admin123, he thong se bat doi ngay.
Dat mat khau tu 8 ky tu tro len, khong bat dau bang admin hay 123456.

He thong SE TU CHOI chay o che do internet neu admin con dung
mat khau admin123. Day la chu y.


BUOC 2. CHAY HE THONG O CHE DO INTERNET
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tat he thong dang chay: dong cua so den, hoac go

    taskkill /F /IM node.exe

Roi bam dup file:

    CHAY-INTERNET.bat

File nay tu sinh khoa bi mat luu vao khoa-bi-mat.txt, bat cac lop
bao ve danh cho internet, roi khoi dong he thong.

Man hinh phai hien:  Che do: INTERNET

LUU Y: file khoa-bi-mat.txt khong duoc gui cho ai. Ai co file nay
co the gia mao phien dang nhap cua quan tri.


BUOC 3. CAI CLOUDFLARE TUNNEL
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Vao trang:  https://one.dash.cloudflare.com
2. Dang ky tai khoan mien phi (dung email cong ty)
3. Ben trai chon:  Networks  ->  Tunnels
4. Bam:  Create a tunnel
5. Chon:  Cloudflared
6. Dat ten, vi du:  qlcd-xaylapmo
7. Bam Save

Man hinh hien ra mot dong lenh dai bat dau bang:

    cloudflared.exe service install eyJhIjoi...

BAM NUT COPY o goc dong do.


BUOC 4. CHAY LENH VUA COPY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Tai cloudflared cho Windows tai:
   https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflared/releases/latest
   Chon file:  cloudflared-windows-amd64.exe

2. Doi ten file vua tai thanh:  cloudflared.exe

3. Chep no vao thu muc  D:\qlcd

4. Mo Command Prompt BANG QUYEN QUAN TRI
   (bam phim Windows, go cmd, chuot phai chon Run as administrator)

5. Go:
       cd /d D:\qlcd

6. Dan dong lenh da copy o Buoc 3, bam Enter

Quay lai trang Cloudflare, trang thai tunnel se doi thanh
CONNECTED mau xanh.


BUOC 5. TRO DIA CHI WEB VE HE THONG
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Van o trang Cloudflare, bam Next.

Muc Public Hostname dien:

    Subdomain:  qlcd
    Domain:     chon ten mien cua anh
    Type:       HTTP
    URL:        localhost:3000

Neu chua co ten mien rieng, Cloudflare cho dung ten mien mien phi
dang  ....trycloudflare.com  de thu truoc.

Bam Save.


BUOC 6. KIEM TRA
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mo dien thoai, TAT WIFI, dung 4G, vao dia chi vua tao.
Vi du:  https://qlcd.tenmiencuaanh.com

Phai thay trang dang nhap va o khoa HTTPS tren thanh dia chi.

Kiem tra them:
  [ ] Dang nhap duoc bang 4G
  [ ] Thu sai mat khau 5 lan -> bi khoa tam 15 phut
  [ ] Tat may tinh cong ty -> khong ai vao duoc (dung nhu thiet ke)
  [ ] Bat lai may -> vao duoc tro lai


BUOC 7. THU HEP LAI CHO AN TOAN
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Da ra internet thi ai cung go duoc dia chi. Nen gioi han lai:

Dang nhap admin -> vao muc BAO MAT TRUY CAP

  1. Bam "Them dai dang dung" de them mang cong ty
  2. Them dai VPN hoac dai 4G cua can bo neu can
  3. Bam "Bat loc"

He thong se khong cho bat neu dia chi cua chinh anh chua nam trong
dai nao, de tranh tu khoa minh ra ngoai.

LUU Y: mang 4G va cap quang gia dinh thuong doi dia chi IP.
Neu can bo di cong truong hay bi chan, thi dung bat loc, ma dua
vao mat khau manh cong voi co che khoa khi do mat khau.


BUOC 8. DE MAY TU CHAY KHI BAT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cloudflared da tu chay nen khi cai bang lenh service install.
Con he thong QLCD thi cai them:

Mo Command Prompt bang quyen quan tri:

    cd /d D:\qlcd
    npm install -g pm2 pm2-windows-startup
    pm2-startup install
    pm2 start server.js --name qlcd
    pm2 save

Nhung pm2 khong doc duoc bien moi truong tu CHAY-INTERNET.bat.
Phai dat bien vinh vien:

    setx QLCD_INTERNET 1 /M
    setx QLCD_SECRET "dan-noi-dung-file-khoa-bi-mat.txt-vao-day" /M

Roi khoi dong lai may.


========================================================================
  VIEC CAN CAN NHAC TRUOC KHI LAM
========================================================================

He thong nay chua nguyen gia tai san, ho so thiet bi va nhan su
phan xuong cua mot don vi thuoc TKV.

Truoc khi mo ra internet, nen hoi bo phan CNTT cua Cong ty. Nhieu
don vi nha nuoc co quy dinh rieng ve du lieu noi bo dua ra ngoai.

Neu chi can vai nguoi truy cap tu xa, dung VPN cua Cong ty se an
toan hon nhieu ma van lam duoc viec: nguoi dung noi VPN vao mang
cong ty roi vao he thong nhu dang ngoi o co quan.

Cloudflare Tunnel phu hop khi:
  - Da hoi y kien va duoc dong y
  - Can nhieu nguoi truy cap tu nhieu noi
  - Khong co VPN san
